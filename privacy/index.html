Privacy

Effective October 21, 2026

On your phone

Your name, goal, reminder time, check-ins, routine and any workout summaries you add are stored on your iPhone. You can export your data or clear your history from your profile.

Your crews

Crews use Apple's CloudKit public database. Anyone who has a crew's six-character code can see that crew. When you start or join a crew, the app shares with that crew:

  • the crew's name and the name of the person who started it
  • your display name, goal emoji and goal title
  • your streak, loaves this week, total loaves and the last day you got bread
  • check-ins from today or yesterday, with Loafy's line for them (the feed shows the last week)
  • nudges you send, with your display name

You're identified by a random iCloud user ID, not your Apple Account email or phone number. Workout details, finish times, your routine and photos are never uploaded. Leaving a crew deletes your card from it. Clearing your history deletes the check-ins you shared.

Hiding and reporting

Hiding a crewmate happens only on your phone. Reporting a crewmate opens an email to us with the crew code and the crewmate's display name and ID; we use it only to review the report.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Apple's push service delivers crew check-ins, nudges and Loafy's daily reminder. Reminders are scheduled on your phone.

Camera and photos

The bread selfie uses your camera or a photo you choose. Face detection runs on your phone to place Loafy. The photo is not uploaded; it leaves your phone only if you share or save it. Saving asks for permission to add the image to Photos.

Apple Health

Connecting Apple Health is optional. The app reads running workouts and distance only to suggest a run for your daily check-in. Nothing is written to Health, and Health data is never uploaded to crews or CloudKit.

No ads or tracking

Get That Bread has no advertising, tracking or third-party analytics. Your data is not sold. Apple may process crash diagnostics under its own privacy policy if you opt in.

Deleting your data

Leave your crews and clear your history in the app to remove what you shared. To ask us to delete anything else, email jeremy.olken@gmail.com.

Children

Get That Bread is not directed to children under 13.
