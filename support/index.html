Help with Get That Bread.

Change your name, goal or reminder

On Today, tap your profile circle in the top right. Change your name, goal and days per week, or turn Loafy's daily reminder on or off, then tap Save. Tap the date and goal in the top left to see your history.

Start, join or switch crews

Open Crew. Tap + to start a crew (give it a name) or join one with a code. If someone texted you an invite, tap Paste and the app finds the code. You can be in up to five crews; tap a crew's name at the top to switch.

Invite friends

In Crew, tap Invite. Share the six-character code, or tap Send invite to text it. Friends need the app, then paste the invite in Crew.

Rename a crew

Whoever started a crew can rename it: tap the crew's name (with the pencil), or tap ⋯ → Rename crew. Everyone in the crew sees the new name.

Hide or report a crewmate

Long-press a crewmate in the League or Feed. Hide removes them from your crews on this phone. Report emails us with the crew and crewmate so we can look into it. To undo hiding, tap ⋯ → Unhide everyone. To leave a crew, tap ⋯ → Leave crew.

Bread selfie

After you check in, tap Text the crew, then Selfie. Take a selfie or choose a photo; Loafy stands next to your face. Tap the arrows to move him, then Send to crew or Save.

Connect Apple Health

Open your profile and tap Connect Apple Health. Only running workouts you allow are read, and you decide whether to add one to a check-in. Manage access in iPhone Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices.

Export or clear your data

Open your profile and scroll to Your data. Export saves a copy through the share sheet. Clear history removes your saved check-ins and the check-ins you shared with your crews.
